## 3. Pricing & How MYSTES Makes Money

MYSTES delivers the **lowest prices in the market** on flights, hotels, and travel services. By searching across multiple provider channels simultaneously and leveraging geographic pricing intelligence, MYSTES consistently finds prices lower than what consumers see on Google Flights, Expedia, or any other retail OTA.

### The Savings-Share Model

MYSTES operates on a simple principle: **the platform only earns when it saves you money.** If there are no savings, there is no fee.

When MYSTES finds a price lower than the retail market benchmark:
- The consumer pays the **MYSTES price** (always lower than retail)
- MYSTES keeps a **percentage of the savings** as the platform fee
- The consumer keeps the rest

```
Example: NYC → London round-trip

Retail market price (Google Flights):  $847
MYSTES price:                          $683
Your savings:                          $164
```

What the consumer sees: a lower price than anywhere else, with a savings badge showing how much they saved. Clean, simple, no complexity.

### Fee Tiers

| Tier | Platform Fee | Who |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Free users** | 50% of savings | Anyone, no account needed |
| **Travel+ subscribers** | 35% of savings | $9.99/mo ($79.99/yr) |
| **B2B Starter** | 25% of savings | Agencies, $49/mo |
| **B2B Growth** | 20% of savings | High-volume agencies, $99/mo |
| **B2B Volume** | 15% of savings | Enterprise agencies, $199/mo |

**Critical rules:**
- **$3 minimum fee** — platform always earns at least $3 per transaction
- **NO maximum fee cap** — fees scale with savings, no ceiling
- **No minimum savings threshold** — we sell tickets regardless of savings amount
- MYSTES only makes money when the consumer saves money

## 4. Travel Intelligence — Multi-Source Search

MYSTES doesn't rely on a single source for pricing. The platform searches **every available channel simultaneously** and delivers the cheapest option for each unique itinerary.

### How It Works

Multiple API providers are queried in parallel. Each provider offers different pricing based on their airline contracts, NDC direct connections, and geographic market access. The same flight can be priced differently across providers — sometimes dramatically.

### API Sources

| Source | Type | Coverage | Unique Value |
|--------|------|----------|-------------|
| **Picasso/Redbox** | GDS Consolidator | Amadeus backend, 102-country POS | Geographic pricing intelligence |
| **Duffel** | NDC Direct | 300+ airlines | NDC-exclusive fares unavailable in GDS |
| **AirGateway** | NDC + GDS Arbitrage | 25+ NDC-direct airlines, 102-country POS | Only API offering both NDC-direct AND geographic arbitrage |
| **Kiwi Tequila** | Virtual Interlining | 750+ carriers | Self-transfer connections other APIs miss |
| **SerpAPI** | Google Flights | All Google-indexed fares | Retail market benchmarking |
| **liteAPI** | Hotel Wholesale | 2M+ properties worldwide | Trade-rate hotel pricing |

### The Deduplication Funnel

```
Picasso Results (50 flights) ──┐
Duffel Results (40 flights)  ──┤
AirGateway Results (30 flights)┤──→ ANASTASiA Dedup ──→ 45 unique itineraries
Kiwi Results (35 flights)    ──┤                         (cheapest per route)
                               │
                    155 raw results → 45 deduplicated
```

For each unique itinerary (same airline, same times, same route), the engine keeps only the cheapest source. Same flight available from three providers at different prices? The consumer sees the lowest.

### Smart Market Selection

The system maintains mappings for 100+ major airports and 50+ country-specific market configurations. It automatically detects the optimal search parameters for each route — because the same flight can be priced very differently depending on where you search from.

### Competitive Price Comparison

Every search result includes a comparison against Google Flights and other major OTAs:
- Real-time Google Flights benchmark via SerpAPI (6-hour Redis cache)
- Competitor breakdown on card selection (Expedia, Priceline, airline direct)
- Bar chart visualization showing MYSTES price vs market
- Savings badge on every result card

### Shared Cart & Payment Splitting

Every item gets flexible assignment — WHO uses it AND WHO pays:

| Split Method | Use Case |
|-------------|----------|
| **Even split** | Default. Room for 2 = 50/50. Tour for 4 = 25 each. |
| **Custom amounts** | "I'll cover $600, you do $420" |
| **One person pays** | Parent treating family, corporate card, partner gift |
| **Percentage** | "60/40 split" with slider interface |

**Key distinction**: `user_id` = who's USING the item (whose ticket, whose room). `payer_id` = who's PAYING (can differ). Mom treats family? Assigned to kids, 100% Mom pays. Corporate flight? Assigned to employee, company card pays.

**Checkout Flow:**
1. Owner taps "Finalize" → cart generates per-person breakdown
2. Each member sees ONLY their portion with full transparency
3. Each member approves → pays from their own payment method
4. OR one person can "treat" selected items for others
5. Confirmations sent to each person's individual dashboard

Nobody sees anyone else's payment details.

## 9. MYSTES Rewards — Cross-Vertical Currency

MYSTES Rewards is not a traditional loyalty program bolted onto a booking engine. It is a **cross-vertical currency** designed to drive discovery, retention, and lock-in across the entire ecosystem.

### Core Economics

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Earn rate** | 10 points per $1 spent on total booking value |
| **Travel+ multiplier** | 1.5x (15 points per $1) |
| **Redemption value** | $0.001 per point (1,000 points = $1) |
| **Point expiry** | 12 months from last earning activity |
| **Reset trigger** | ANY new booking resets clock on ALL points |

### Earning Points

| Action | Points Earned |
|--------|--------------|
| Every $1 spent on bookings | 10 points (base) |
| First booking ever | 5,000 bonus |
| Refer a friend who books | 2,000 bonus |
| Leave a review after trip | 500 bonus |
| 2-vertical bundle (flight + hotel) | 1.5x multiplier on entire bundle |
| 3+ vertical bundle | 2.0x multiplier on entire bundle |

Bundle multipliers are the key — they incentivize multi-vertical booking, which is where the ecosystem creates maximum value and maximum lock-in.

### Streak Bonuses

| Streak | Bonus |
|--------|-------|
| 2 months consecutive | 500 points |
| 3 months consecutive | 1,000 points |
| 6 months consecutive | 5,000 points + **Gold** badge |
| 12 months consecutive | 15,000 points + **Platinum** badge |

### Redemption Caps (Margin Protection)

| Vertical | Max Points Credit Per Booking |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Flights | 30% of booking value |
| Hotels | 30% of booking value |
| Activities/Experiences | **100% (full cover)** |
| Subscription month | 1 free month per quarter max |
| Minimum redemption | 1,000 points ($1) |

### The Commission Waiver Model

This is the economic insight that makes rewards brilliant: when a user redeems points for an activity (like a Viator tour), MYSTES doesn't pay cash — it simply **waives its commission** on that booking. The cost to MYSTES is zero dollars. The perceived value to the user is $50+ for a "free" experience.

| Vertical | Typical Commission | "Free" Cost to MYSTES |
|----------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Viator experience ($50 tour) | ~8-15% = $4-7 | $0 cash (forgone commission) |
| Hotel upgrade | ~8% margin | $0 cash (forgone margin) |
| Travel insurance | ~20-30% = $10-15 | $0 cash (forgone commission) |
| Airport transfer | ~10-15% | $0 cash (forgone commission) |

Never spending real money. Trading forgone commission on bookings that wouldn't have happened otherwise. The user discovers a new vertical through points, loves it, books paid next time.

### Cross-Vertical Currency

Points earned on flights can be redeemed for hotel stays, activities, car rentals — any vertical. This creates the discovery loop:
- User books a flight → earns 8,000 points
- ANASTASiA: "You have enough points for a free walking tour in Rome"
- User discovers activities vertical → books paid activities on next trip
- Each vertical makes every other vertical stickier

### Point Gifting

| Rule | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Who can gift | Any user with 5,000+ points balance |
| Who can receive | Any registered MYSTES user |
| Monthly send limit | 20,000 points per sender |
| Monthly receive limit | 50,000 points per recipient |
| Minimum gift | 1,000 points ($1) |
| Fee | None |
| Family pooling | Shared household, max 5 members |

**Prohibited**: Open trading, point selling, cash-out, point brokers.

### B2B Team Points Pool

- Agencies accumulate points across ALL bookings into shared team pool
- Can use for: subscription credits, client incentives, team rewards, booking discounts
- High-volume agencies earn free subscription months automatically
- **Switching platforms = abandoning accrued rewards** — deepens lock-in
