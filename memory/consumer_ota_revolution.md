## 7. Trip Planner as Distribution Channel

### The Insight
Trip Planner isn't a collaboration feature. It's a **distribution channel disguised as a collaboration tool.** Every group trip planned on MYSTES is a sales event that MYSTES doesn't organize, market, or manage. The customer does it for you.

### How It Works
- B2B subscriber creates trip plan (e.g., "Senior Trip to Cancun")
- Invites 25 classmates via link
- Each classmate books at the B2B subscriber's rate (25% instead of 50%)
- Each classmate creates an account (required to join plan)
- B2B subscriber earns commission on every booking
- Several classmates subscribe to Travel+ at checkout

### Economics (25-person trip, $100 savings per ticket):
- B2B subscriber earns: 25 × 5,000 points = 125,000 points ($125)
- Subscription cost: $49/mo
- Net profit: $76 for planning a trip they were going to plan anyway
- MYSTES earns: 25 × $25 fee = $625 + subscription revenue + new accounts

### Customer Acquisition Cost: NEGATIVE
The B2B subscriber PAYS YOU to bring you customers. This is unprecedented.

## 8. B2B as Franchise Model

### The Franchise Analogy
A B2B subscriber with 50 referrals is a franchise owner. They don't own the platform — they own their **customer relationships.** As long as their subscription is active, every referral booking generates income.

### Why the Subscription Never Gets Canceled
- 50 referrals × 2-3 bookings/year = 100-150 bookings
- At 5,000 points per booking = 500,000-750,000 points ($500-750/year)
- Subscription cost: $588/year ($49 × 12)
- Net profit: -$88 to +$162/year from points ALONE
- Plus any direct commission mechanisms on top

The subscription isn't a cost. It's rent on a business.

### Two Doors for Every Consumer

**Door 1 — Direct:** Book on MYSTES at guest/member/Travel+ rates (50%/45%/35%)

**Door 2 — Through a B2B referral:** Book via friend's link at B2B rate (25%/20%/15%)

Consumer prefers Door 2 (cheaper). B2B subscriber prefers Door 2 (they earn). MYSTES prefers Door 2 (volume + subscription + engagement). Everyone wins through Door 2.

### NOT MLM — Key Distinctions
1. Flat structure — earn from DIRECT referrals only, no pyramid levels
2. No inventory — nobody buys product to resell
3. No recruitment pressure — earn from BOOKINGS, not from signing up B2B subscribers
4. Consumer always has direct access — Door 1 is always available
5. Product is genuinely cheaper — not overpriced goods sold to friends

## 16. Google Review Weapon (Build #178)

### The Mechanism
Every booking generates a **pre-built savings review card** — the customer doesn't write anything. The card contains:

- Real flight details (route, airline, dates)
- MYSTES price paid
- Competitor prices (Google Flights lowest: Expedia, Kayak, Kiwi, etc. via SerpAPI)
- Savings amount and percentage
- Travel+ subscription value (if applicable)
- Full discount breakdown (base savings → member discount → share bonus → points)

The customer taps "Share & Review" → card posts to Google Reviews automatically. They can add personal thoughts but the DATA is pre-populated. No friction, no writing, no effort.

### Why This Is Lethal
- Every review is a **publicly indexed competitive price comparison**
- Google serves these reviews to the NEXT person searching "cheap flights to [destination]"
- Reviews are REAL customers with REAL savings — not paid testimonials
- The discount (5% share bonus) FUNDS the review — marketing cost = the discount already built into the model
- **Perpetual motion**: Competitors overcharge → reviews show bigger savings → more customers → more reviews
- Cost per review: ~$0.50-1.00 (5% of one booking fee). Expedia pays $5-15 per Google Ad click.

### The Review Card (Standard Template)
```
[MYSTES Logo]
I just saved $[savings] on my flight!

[Origin] → [Destination] | [Airline] [Flight#]
[Date] | [Cabin Class]

MY PRICE: $[mystes_price]

COMPETITOR PRICES:
  Expedia:     $[price]

[Optional: Travel+ member — saving an extra [travel_plus_savings]]

Try MYSTES: mystes.app/ref/[referral_code]
```

### Integration Points
- Post-booking confirmation page: "Share & Review" button
- First flight special: review discount on first booking
- Always available: discount for sharing booking card to Google Review portal
- Share platforms: Google Reviews (primary), plus Twitter/Instagram/WhatsApp/etc.

## 17. Three-Tool Marketing Stack + Referral Card Generator (Build #179)

### The Stack
| Tool | Cost to MYSTES | Incentive | Where |
|------|---------------|-----------|-------|
| Google Review Card | 5% per booking | Discount (always) | Google Reviews |
| Social Share | 2% first / points after | Discount → points | Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp |
| Referral Card | $0 | B2B=revenue, B2C=points | Bio links, stories, DMs |

### Tiered Share Incentives
- **Google Review**: 5% discount EVERY booking (permanent indexed marketing asset, value never diminishes)
- **Social Share — First Booking**: 2% cash discount (onboarding hook, maximum conversion incentive)
- **Social Share — Recurring**: 200 points per share (retention via ecosystem currency, NOT cash)
- **Points after first booking**: Once customer is in the ecosystem, referral system + points handle retention
- Points create lock-in (switching cost). Cash is rigid. Points are tunable.

### Referral Card (The Volume Play)
- MYSTES generates a pre-built visual card showing user's savings stats + referral link
- B2B shares it because bookings through their link = sales revenue for their business
- B2C shares it because bookings through their code = points they redeem across verticals
- Influencers put it in bio/stories because their income scales with follower bookings
- The earning mechanism IS the incentive — MYSTES pays $0 per card, $0 per share, $0 per click
- Card shows: total savings, trips booked, favorite destination, "Join MYSTES" CTA
- Shareable on stories, bios, DMs — zero friction

### Why Three Tools
- **Google Review** = authority play (permanent, indexed, verified)
- **Social Share** = trust play (friend-to-friend, personal network)
- **Referral Card** = volume play (unlimited distribution, self-incentivizing)
- All three feed the same funnel. All three compound. Only one costs real money (5% Google).

### Locked Decisions (Build #179)
28. **Google Review = 5% ALWAYS** — every booking, permanent, never diminishes
29. **Social first = 2% cash** — onboarding hook, maximum conversion
30. **Social recurring = points** — 200 pts per share, NOT cash (retention + lock-in)
31. **Referral Card = $0** — B2B earns revenue, B2C earns points, MYSTES pays nothing
32. **Points after onboarding** — referral system (indefinite, point-based) handles long-term growth
33. **No points for sharing referral cards** — reward is through USAGE (bookings), not distribution
