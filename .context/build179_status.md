## Strategy Decisions Locked

### 1. Three-Tool Marketing Stack ($0 Advertising Budget)
| Tool | Cost to MYSTES | Incentive | Who Uses It | Where |
|------|---------------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Google Review Card | 5% per booking | Discount off booking | Every customer | Google Reviews (indexed forever) |
| Social Share | 2% first / points after | Discount → points | Every customer | Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp |
| Referral Card | $0 | B2B = sales revenue, B2C = points | Everyone | Bio links, stories, DMs |

### 2. Google Review = 5% ALWAYS (Permanent)
- Every booking, every time — because the review is a permanent indexed marketing asset
- Each new review has fresh data, new route, new competitor comparison
- The value NEVER diminishes — Google indexes it forever
- Cost per review: ~$0.50-1.00 (5% of one booking fee). Expedia pays $5-15 per click.

### 3. Social Share = 2% First Booking / Points After
- First social share: 2% cash discount (onboarding hook — maximum conversion)
- Recurring: 200 points per share (retention via ecosystem currency)
- Points, NOT cash, for recurring — creates lock-in (switching cost)
- Points are tunable (controlled redemption value), cash is rigid

### 4. Referral Card = $0 Cost to MYSTES
- Pre-generated visual card with user's savings stats + referral link
- B2B shares because bookings = sales revenue for their business
- B2C shares because bookings = points they can redeem
- Influencers put in bio because their income scales with follower bookings
- The earning mechanism IS the incentive — nobody needs to be PAID to share

### 5. Points After First Booking (NOT Recurring Cash)
- First-time share discount = acquisition tool (cash value, immediate)
- After onboarding, customer is inside the ecosystem
- Referral system (indefinite, point-based) handles long-term viral growth
- Points create lock-in: balance = switching cost
- Points are tunable: MYSTES controls redemption value

### 6. No Points System for Referral Cards
- Referral cards don't award points for SHARING — they award through USAGE
- B2B: gets a sale → earns revenue
- B2C: someone books through referral code → earns points
- Zero cost per card generation, zero cost per share, zero cost per click

## Code Changes (Build #179)

### New: ReferralCard Model
- `ReferralCard` model (models.py) — one per user, stores aggregate savings stats, booking count, referral code, card token, click/conversion tracking

### New: Referral Card Generator Routes
- `POST /api/referral-card/generate` — creates/updates referral card from user's booking history (total bookings, total savings, favorite destination/airline, referral count, points earned)
- `GET /ref-card/<token>` — public referral card page (shareable, visual, tracks clicks)
- `POST /api/referral-card/click/<token>` — explicit click tracking for analytics
- `REFERRAL_CARD_CONTENT` template — branded card with stats grid, savings badge, "Join MYSTES" CTA

### Updated: Tiered Share Discount Logic
- `POST /api/review/<token>/shared` now implements tiered incentives:
  - Google Review: 5% discount ALWAYS (creates SocialShare with discount_applied=True)
  - Social first booking: 2% cash discount (SocialShare with discount_applied=True)
  - Social recurring: 200 points via PointsTransaction (no discount, SocialShare with discount_applied=False)
- Response now includes `reward_type` ('discount' or 'points'), `points_awarded`, and contextual `message`

### Updated: Booking Confirmation
- "Share Your Referral Card" section added after Google Review section
- "Generate & Copy Link" button → calls `/api/referral-card/generate` → copies public URL
- "View Card" button appears after generation
- JS updated to show tiered reward messages from share response
